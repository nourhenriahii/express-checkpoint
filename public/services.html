What I take on

Four areas, one way of thinking.

Development, security, and design aren't separate hats for me — they inform each other on every project I take on.

Software Development

Backend and full-stack builds, written with maintainability and security in mind from the first commit, not patched in before launch.

Cybersecurity

Vulnerability assessments, secure authentication flows, and dependency audits — finding the weak points before someone else does.

UI/UX Design (Figma)

Interfaces prototyped and refined in Figma, grounded in how the product actually behaves rather than how it looks in isolation.

AI-Assisted Tooling

Practical use of AI-generated content and tooling inside real workflows — prompt design, evaluation, and integration, used deliberately rather than as a shortcut.

How it usually goes

A short call first to see if it's a fit. If it is, I send a scope and a timeline before anything is signed. Timelines vary by project — security audits move faster than full builds.
